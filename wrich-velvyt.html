A Letter From the Founder
"Luxury was never meant to scream.
It was meant to recognize itself."

I started Wrich Velvyt in 2017 with one belief: that the most powerful things in the world don't need to announce themselves. They simply are.

We don't make clothes for everyone. We make them for the person who walks into a room and needs nothing from it — who has already decided who they are.

What we build here is not seasonal. It is permanent — the kind of thing passed down, not discarded. We move slowly, deliberately, and only when we have something worth saying.

The Inner Circle is where the collection lives first. Where the decisions are made. Where the world begins.

— William Kingston
