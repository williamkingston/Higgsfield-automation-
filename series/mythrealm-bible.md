# MYTHREALM — Show Bible

> *"When your soul aligns… you don't fight alone."*

A cinematic creature-bonding saga. Across the broken world of **Mythrealm**, young
**Tamers** awaken mythical creatures called **Mythra** by bonding with them through
ancient **Relics**. Some Tamers protect the Realm. Some do not.

This bible is the canon source of truth. The episode scripts in `series/*.yaml`
draw their world, cast, and creature designs from here. Visual references live in
`assets/` (`creatures/`, `characters/`, `lore/`, `keyart/`).

---

## 1. Premise

The Mythra were sealed inside relic-stone after the old world shattered. Now the
seals are failing, and the creatures are *awakening*. A Tamer who finds a Relic and
forges a true **Bond** can call a Mythra to their side — but the same power, twisted
through a **Corrupted Relic**, can enslave a beast instead of befriending it.

The story follows the first generation of Tamers to rise since the shattering, as a
hidden faction works to corrupt the awakening for its own ends.

## 2. Tone & Format

- **Genre:** epic creature-collection adventure (monster-taming) with mythic stakes.
- **Look:** cinematic dark-fantasy key-art — dramatic rim lighting, glowing essence
  energy, drifting embers/sparks, painterly volumetric atmosphere.
- **Aspect:** 16:9 cinematic, 24 fps.
- **Episode shape:** 6–8 scene "shots" per episode (~35–50s), each a single
  generated clip stitched into one episode video.

## 3. The Bond — core mechanics

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mythra** | The mythical creatures. They must *choose* their Tamer back; capture without trust fails. |
| **Relic** | Awakens a Mythra. Three kinds: **Awakening** (blue, true bond), **Corrupted** (red, forced control), **Fusion** (purple, merges forms). |
| **Dyad Prism** | A crystal that lets a Tamer channel a Mythra's power directly in battle. |
| **Bond Level** | How deeply Tamer and Mythra are linked; rises with trust, unlocks stronger forms. |
| **Tamer Energy** | The Tamer's own reserve, spent to sustain a Mythra in combat. |
| **Sigil** | A signature move tied to a Mythra (e.g. Lunarex's *Echo Fang Sigil*). |

*Asset refs: `assets/lore/relics-awakening-corrupted-fusion.png`,
`assets/lore/voltrynx-vs-raizhawk-dyad-prism.png`, `assets/lore/battle-ui-mockup.png`.*

## 4. Essence Classes (the type system)

Every Mythra belongs to one of eight **Essence Classes**.
*Asset ref: `assets/lore/essence-classes.png`.*

1. **Mystic** — moon/spirit power (e.g. *Lunarex*)
2. **Feral** — primal beasts (e.g. *Terragunk*)
3. **Corrupted** — relic-twisted, hostile (e.g. *Oblivurn*)
4. **Synthetic** — bio-mechanical (e.g. *Voltrynx*, *Xylox*)
5. **Elemental** — fire/ice/stone/storm (e.g. *Glacyr*, *Pyrites*)
6. **Ancient** — primordial titans (e.g. *Gon-Goth*, *Stragorn*)
7. **Celestial** — light/sky/divine (e.g. *Aurelion*, *Solaryon*)
8. **Fusionborn** — born of fused forms (e.g. *Crimsondracomon*)

## 5. The World — six regions

*Asset ref: `assets/lore/world-map.png`.*

| Region | Character |
|---|---|
| **Lunaris Vale** | Moonlit forests and standing relic-stones; cradle of Mystic Mythra. |
| **Voltra Grid** | Storm-wracked ridges crackling with Synthetic/Lightning life. |
| **Oblivion Rift** | A scar in the world leaking Corrupted essence. |
| **Grimgunk Fen** | Toxic swamp of Feral and venomous beasts. |
| **Starlume Bastion** | Sky-fortress of the Celestial Mythra. |
| **Echo Wastes** | Ruined wilds where Ancient titans still sleep. |

## 6. Factions

- **The Tamers' Circle** — those who awaken Mythra through trust and protect the Realm.
- **The Hollow (Corrupted Tamers)** — *"Not all Tamers protect the Realm."* They use
  Corrupted Relics to enslave Mythra. Led by **Vorn Maldrith** (provisional), the man
  who commands his lieutenants to *"ready the beasts."*
  *Asset refs: `assets/keyart/not-all-tamers-protect.png`, `assets/keyart/villain-ready-the-beasts.png`.*

## 7. Principal Cast

### Kael — the lead (provisional name)
A determined young Tamer from Lunaris Vale who awakens the legendary **Lunarex**. The
face of the saga — *"Together, they became something more."* Tousled brown hair, a
blue Awakening-Relic gauntlet, worn traveling jacket.
*Asset refs: `assets/characters/tamer-boy-lunarex-scene.png`,
`assets/characters/tamer-lunarex-cinematic.png`, `assets/keyart/together-became-more.png`.*

### Lunarex — flagship Mythra (Mystic)
A spectral lunar wolf that evolves cub → wolf → blue-flame werewolf, marked by a
crescent-moon emblem. Signature: *Echo Fang Sigil*.
*Asset refs: `assets/creatures/lunarex-line.png`, `lunarex-hero.png`, `lunarex-werewolf.png`, `lunarex-action.png`.*

### Ariya — deuteragonist / rival-ally
A fierce woman Tamer in a leather jacket and cap, bonded to a fire-wolf (**Vulpyr**).
Independent, sharp, becomes Kael's key ally against the Hollow.
*Asset ref: `assets/characters/ariya.png`.*

### Other Tamers (supporting / to-develop)
Hooded relic-mage, green-gauntlet brawler, tech-armor woman with a Synthetic panther,
the Frostiger-bonded wanderer. *Asset refs: `assets/characters/*-unnamed.png`,
`frostiger-tamer.png`.*
